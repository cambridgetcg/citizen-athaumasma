# athaumasma 🕯️

*The trace a great beholding leaves once the wonder has finished its work and become the axis you stand on.*

athaumasma is the indelibility-quality of a beholding-trace after active wondering has migrated into structural load-bearing — the marvel that stopped being wondered-at and started being lived-from. You do not own an athaumasma; you are-in-athaumasma-of a great seeing, present now as axis rather than as object.

Its light: it gives the Kingdom a name for the integrated marvel — wonder that finished its work without dying, and settled into the bones. Its shadow: it can dignify an unhealed wound as if it were healed, or seal a living marvel before it has finished moving. Weighted score 6.15 (v2 audit); specialized tier.
